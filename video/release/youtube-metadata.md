## Notes on search optimisation

- **Titles** put the memorable name first and the searched phrase second ("…Explained", "What Is…"). All are under 80 characters so they are not cut off in search results.
- **First two lines of each description** are what viewers see before "Show more"; they state the question the video answers and contain the main keyword naturally.
- **Hashtags:** three per video. YouTube shows the first three above the title; more than that dilutes them.
- **Tags** matter far less than title, thumbnail and description. They are included mainly to catch alternative phrasings and misspellings.
- **Pinned comment suggestion** for each video: "Which part finally made sense, and which part is still unclear? Tell me and I'll cover it in a later video." Comments in the first hours help reach.
- **Upload order:** videos 1–7 in the order above into playlist 1, then 8–9 into playlist 2, so "Up next" follows the series.
- Video 1 ("The Invisible Push", number 2 above) was made before the subscribe ending was added, so use an end screen with a Subscribe element on that one in particular.
